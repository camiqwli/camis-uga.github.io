Research Areas

AI & Autonomy
Probing Large Language Models for Autonomous Driving Behavior
Investigating how LLMs can be leveraged to interpret and navigate complex real-world driving scenarios, including edge cases and ambiguous traffic conditions.
Autonomous Systems
LLM-Assisted AV Recovery from Immobilization
Developing AI-powered recovery protocols for autonomous vehicles that become stuck or immobilized in challenging on-road environments.
Sustainability
Mixed Wireless Charging Network Optimization for Electric Buses
Optimizing the deployment and scheduling of wireless in-motion charging infrastructure to maximize fleet electrification efficiency for public transit.
Sensing & Safety
LiDAR & Camera Data Analytics for Traffic Safety Management
Fusing multi-modal sensor data streams to detect safety-critical events, monitor vulnerable road users, and inform proactive traffic management decisions.
Traffic Flow
Autonomous Vehicle Interaction with Traffic Signs & Flow Impacts
Analyzing how AV compliance and decision-making at signalized and unsignalized intersections shapes macroscopic traffic flow dynamics at scale.
Testbed Development
Full-Scale & Reduced-Scale CAV Testbed Development
Building physical and digital testbed environments — from scaled model tracks to full-scale vehicle platforms — to validate connected and automated mobility algorithms.
Infrastructure
Infrastructure Evaluation for Autonomous Vehicles
Assessing existing roadway infrastructure readiness for AV deployment and identifying upgrades needed to support safe, efficient autonomous operations.

03

Teaching

CVLE 8210
Traffic Flow Theory

An in-depth exploration of highway traffic flow covering foundational concepts and a diversity of models. Examines traffic dynamics from both individual vehicle (microscopic) and overall traffic stream (macroscopic) viewpoints, offering insights into various traffic flow theories.

CVLE 8120
Transportation Planning

Equips students with knowledge and skills to analyze, design, and implement effective transportation systems. Topics include land use, environmental impact, multimodal systems, and policy frameworks, with a focus on creating sustainable and equitable transportation solutions.

CVLE 4230/6230
Transportation Safety

A comprehensive overview of transportation safety, focusing on key concepts, theories, and management strategies. Covers human factors and the behavior of vulnerable road users. Through a blend of theory and practical applications, students are equipped to improve transportation safety effectively.
